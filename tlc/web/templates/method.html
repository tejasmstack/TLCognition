What a number means here

  • measured (chip m) — derived from this image's pixels by deterministic code. Same bytes, same result, byte for byte.
  • chosen (c) — a configured constant or an operator input. A decision, not an observation.
  • inferred (i) — derived from measured values under a stated model.
  • refused (r) — the system declined to produce the value; the reason and the remedy are shown in its place.

Detection

A band is counted when at least {{ (op.tiers.reported.agreement_min * 100)|round(0)|int }}% of {{ grid.configs|length }} independent processing variants ({{ grid.id }}) find a peak within tolerance, and the median surrogate-null p-value is at most {{ op.tiers.reported.p_med_max }}. Bands between {{ (op.tiers.candidate.agreement_min * 100)|round(0)|int }}% and the threshold are listed below the detection threshold — not counted. The agreement tally is ordinal evidence, not a probability.

Rst, Rf and the solvent front

No plate in this lab's corpus carries a drawn solvent front (F2), so Rf is not reported. Positions are reported as Rst: the distance from the origin relative to the highest confirmed band in the standard lane. Without a standard lane, positions are reported as a fraction of plate height and are comparable within that plate only.

Photometry and clipping

Areas are reported only when less than {{ '%.0f'|format(100 * 0.15) }}% of the in-plate green channel is saturated; above {{ '%.0f'|format(100 * 0.40) }}% nothing is measured. Optical density is log10(I0/I) against a poly3 background; noise σ is measured once on the raw analysable band before spot masking.

Detection limit statement

For an empty lane, the faintest band that would have registered is reported as {{ z_limit|int }}σ of the lane's OD noise — a chosen factor, stated so a null result is a bounded statement.

Open gates

  • Gate 4 (detection recall ≥ 0.95 on synthetic plates): not met — recall ≈ 0.93; false-positive arm met on synthetic-noise blanks only.
  • Gate 5 (position p95 ≤ 1% of plate height; false-streak ≤ 2%): not met — 1.3% and 5%.
  • Gates 6–7 (human labels; calibration): not started — this is why no confidence is shown.

What a number means here

  • measured m — derived from this image's pixels by deterministic code. Same bytes, same result, byte for byte.
  • chosen c — a configured constant or an operator input. A decision, not an observation.
  • inferred i — derived from measured values under a stated model.
  • refused r — the system declined to produce the value; the reason and the remedy appear in its place.

Detection

A band is counted when at least {{ (op.tiers.reported.agreement_min * 100)|round(0)|int }}% of {{ grid.configs|length }} independent processing variants ({{ grid.id }}) find a peak within tolerance, and the median surrogate-null p-value is at most {{ op.tiers.reported.p_med_max }}. Bands between {{ (op.tiers.candidate.agreement_min * 100)|round(0)|int }}% and that bar are listed under Below the detection threshold — not counted, never hidden. The agreement tally is ordinal evidence, not a probability.

Rst, Rf and the solvent front

No plate in this lab's corpus carries a drawn solvent front, so Rf is not reported. Positions are reported as Rst: the distance from the origin relative to the highest confirmed band in the standard lane. Without a standard lane, positions are reported as a fraction of plate height and are comparable within that plate only.

Photometry and clipping

Areas are reported only when less than 15% of the in-plate green channel is saturated; above 40% nothing is measured. Optical density is log10(I₀/I) against a poly3 background, and the noise σ is measured once on the raw analysable band before any spot masking.

Detection limit statement

For an empty lane, the faintest band that would have registered is reported as {{ z_limit|int }}σ of the lane's optical-density noise — a chosen factor, stated so that a null result is a bounded statement rather than silence.

What that factor is worth, measured on 756 held-out synthetic spots at the shipped operating point: detection rate 87.5% for spots of 5–6σ, 94.8% for 6–8σ, 98.7% for 8–12σ and 100% above 12σ. A band at the stated limit is found about seven times in eight, not certainly; a band twice that size is found essentially always.
